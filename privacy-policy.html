Privacy Policy

Centre Youth Android App
Keezhillam - Munnar Centre Mar Thoma Yuvajana Sakhyam • Effective Date: November 7, 2026
Summary for Google Play & Users: The Centre Youth app respects your privacy completely. We do not collect, harvest, track, sell, or share personal data. No registration or account is required. The app is completely safe, ad-free, and designed for family and youth church fellowship.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the Centre Youth mobile application (Package: org.marthoma.centreyouth) developed by Jeremiah Godly Varghese for Keezhillam - Munnar Centre Mar Thoma Yuvajana Sakhyam (Kottayam - Kochi Diocese, Malankara Mar Thoma Syrian Church).

The application provides members, youth fellowship delegates, and church visitors with liturgical lectionary readings, youth fellowship songs, camp announcements, leadership contacts, and parish directories.

2. Data Collection & Privacy Principles

  • Zero Mandatory Personal Information (PII): We do not require account registration, passwords, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, or location coordinates.
  • No Account Required: All liturgical readings, hymns, Bible quiz levels, and directory entries are openly accessible without requiring sign-in or login credentials.
  • Optional Community Submissions (Prayer Wall & Quiz Leaderboard): Users may optionally choose to submit a prayer request (anonymously or with an optional display name and parish) or share their Bible Quiz score to the fellowship leaderboard. These voluntary submissions are transmitted securely via HTTPS and stored solely in the official Centre Sakhyam Google Sheet for display to fellowship members. They are never sold, commercialized, or used for tracking or marketing.
  • No Advertising or Tracking: The application does not contain third-party advertising SDKs, commercial ad trackers, or behavioural analytics frameworks.
  • No Data Sharing with Third Parties: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose any user data to third parties.

3. Permissions & Technical Disclosures

The application requests only minimal, transparent permissions necessary to perform its advertised features:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+ / API 33+):
    Usage: Used strictly to deliver an optional daily morning scripture reminder (at 7:00 AM) and liturgical Sunday lectionary alerts. You can freely grant or deny this permission, or turn it off at any time in device Settings.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK (Android 14+ / API 34+):
    Usage: Used strictly to play Centre Sakhyam youth songs continuously in the background when you switch apps or turn off your device screen. A system media playback notification with play, pause, and seek controls is displayed while audio is active.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE:
    Usage: Used solely to stream audio tracks from the official Sakhyam Google Drive audio archive and verify network availability.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED:
    Usage: Used solely to re-register the scheduled daily scripture alarm with Android's WorkManager when your phone restarts.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

The application is suitable for general audiences, youth, and families. It complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. All content is educational, religious, and community-focused.

5. Local Device Storage

Any preferences you save inside the app (such as favorite songs, preferred Malayalam or English language display, or dark mode) are stored strictly on your local device using Android SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device and is wiped immediately if you uninstall the application.

6. Security Measures

All network communications (such as audio streaming and external scripture links) utilize industry-standard Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS) encryption to protect against interception and unauthorized tampering.

7. Policy Changes

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory adjustments. Any modifications will be posted to this page with an updated effective date.
